# Limitations and claims policy

Alight measures canary-class transactions from one collection host. A route,
region, time window, workload class, interval, effective sample count and age
scope each quote. Higher requested compute and extra accounts do not reproduce
a contended swap. Observer receive latency includes transport lag. Monotonic
timestamps are comparable only within the recorded process clock identity.

Beam HTTP and plain RPC use the configured Solami RPC endpoint; tipped versus
untipped is a workload distinction, not evidence of independent HTTP backends.
QUIC is separate. No funded owned route landing, live N=40 Prove, mainnet memo
anchor or complete four-observer agreement has been verified in this work.
Collection remains paused. Earlier observe-only records are not canary outcomes.

Missing observations remain unknown. A timeout is never expiry. Proven expiry
requires passed last-valid block height and covered commitment-level RPC
absence; conflicting candidates retain block identity and disagreement evidence.
Webhook/index coverage may prevent complete observer agreement. Observer lag
alone cannot establish a network regime change. Historical steps demonstrated
in simulation do not establish mainnet upgrades or Alpenglow activation.

Full exploration has 81 cells. Lean has 10 Small cells and omits Medium/Large
and X2/X10 tiers. Lean-only records cannot support the registered four-tier
Beam Signal analysis. Do not mix profile populations without checking the
analysis and propensities. Effective n is discounted resolved evidence, not
raw traffic or prior counts. Small samples and stale data refuse a recommendation.
Null/FLAT/INCONCLUSIVE Signal and unfavorable Prove verdicts remain visible.
Forecasts crossing regime changes are voided through append-only grades.

Passive tape and wallet captures see selected landed executions and miss
unlanded submissions. They have survivorship and filter/retention bias. Visible
failed share is not a submission failure rate. Wallet transport and chain time
are not inferred from recipients or receive time. Unknown payments stay unknown;
missing contemporaneous frontiers are not replaced with today's quote. Spend
above a supported tip is conditional arithmetic, not proven waste or proof of
equal swap performance. The Data API wallet-history adapter remains unverified.

Blur economics depend on user-supplied size, edge, adverse-selection factor and
SOL conversion plus sufficiently recent pool evidence. Combining pool returns
with canary landing assumes the workload is representative. Estimates are not
a trading signal, guaranteed savings or financial advice. Stale/sparse market
data fall back to probability-only quotes.

Exports contain retained source-scoped evidence, not complete chain history.
Live, Sim and Replay are separate populations. The downloadable example is
Sim and has insufficient cells; it demonstrates schemas and verification.
An unsigned anchor draft is not an on-chain timestamp or proof of landing.
Hash chains establish byte integrity, not truth or guaranteed predictive power.
No claim of being first, having no competitors, validator-level prediction,
universal route superiority, workload transfer or guaranteed landing is made.
